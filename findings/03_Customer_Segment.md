## CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION AND Q4MARKETING FOCUS
We segmented customers based on the number of delivered orders during the analysis period:

- No-orders: 0 delivered orders
- One-Time Buyer: 1 delivered order
- Repeat Buyer: 2–4 delivered orders
- VIP Buyer: 5+ delivered orders

Out of 10,000 customers:
 3,034 (30.34%) are One-Time Buyers 
 1,893 (18.93%) are Repeat Buyers
 1,125 (11.25%) are VIP Buyers. 
 3,948 (39.48%) no orders 

Returning customers contribute substantially more revenue than one-time customers.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MARKETING?

Repeat and VIP customers together represent 30.18% of the customer base but contribute 84.61% of delivered revenue. This indicates that returning customers are a major source of customer value.

For Q4, marketing should focus on two areas:

1. Retain and grow VIP customers. VIP Buyers account for 11.25% of customers but generate 56.85% of revenue. Retention, loyalty, and 
   targeted cross-sell or upsell initiatives can help protect this high-value segment.

2. Move Repeat Buyers toward VIP status. Repeat Buyers already demonstrate continued purchasing behavior and contribute 27.76% of revenue. 
   Targeted offers and loyalty initiatives could encourage higher purchase frequency.

One-Time Buyers represent 30.34% of the customer base but only 15.39% of revenue. They provide a sizeable pool for reactivation and conversion into Repeat Buyers, but their current value is lower than that of returning customers.

Best Customer Segment
For this analysis, we define the "best customer segment" as the segment with the highest lifetime value and revenue contribution.

By this definition, VIP Buyers are the highest-value segment. They average 10.19 delivered orders and $76,007.30 in lifetime spend per customer, while generating 56.85% of total delivered revenue.

## METHODOLOGY NOTE
All segmentation metrics are based on **delivered orders** using the normalized order status (LOWER(TRIM(status)) = 'delivered'). The analysis uses order count to define segments and does not distinguish how much time passes between purchases.
