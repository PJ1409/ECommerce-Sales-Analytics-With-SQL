## ABOUT BUSINESS

The business is a multi-category ecommerce retailer selling products across four main categories: Electronics, Apparel, Beauty, and Home. Its catalog covers a broad range of products, from headphones and smartwatches to clothing, skincare, kitchen products, and home furnishings.
The retailer carries 4,000 products sourced from 4,000 brands across 18 product categories, of which 3,867 (96.7%) are currently active. The active assortment is fairly distributed across the four main categories:

- Electronics: 1,106 active products
- Apparel: 1,089 active products
- Beauty: 867 active products
- Home: 805 active products

## SCALE
The business has 10,000 registered customers, of which 8,800 have placed at least one order, while the remaining customers are registered but have not yet made a purchase.

8,800 Customers have placed 40,000 orders, averaging approximately 4 orders per purchasing customer,and average of approximately 2.05 items per order.demonstrating healthy repeat purchase behaviour.

The available data covers customer purchases from 16 March to 14 June 2026, providing roughly three months of business activity to explore.

During this period, the business generated approximately ₹298.75 million (₹29.88 crore) in revenue with an Average Revenue generated per Customer of ₹33,949.33 (Numbers based on all the distinct orders)

## HOW CUSTOMERS SHOP

Customers purchase across a broad range of categories rather than concentrating heavily in a single category. Skincare category has the highest number of distinct orders, with 3,011 and 2,024 orders respectively, while bedding has the lowest at 2,428 orders (Numbers based on only Delivered orders). Overall, the category mix indicates relatively diversified customer demand.

Across the full dataset, there are approximately 4.55 orders per customer. This indicates repeat ordering, although this overall ratio does not mean that every customer places the same number of orders.

Monthly activity provides additional context. April was the strongest month, with 17,200 orders from 5,102 customers. Orders per customer also reached 3.37 in April, compared with 3.05 in March and 2.33 in May. This suggests that April's increase was driven not only by having more active customers, but also by customers placing orders more frequently.

## OBSERVATIONS

While the dataset is generally well‑structured and suitable for business analysis, several missing details pose data quality challenges. Core identifiers such as customer IDs are intact, but it is important to address these data quality issues in advance to ensure reliable insights

The business shows broad-based ecommerce demand and evidence of repeat purchasing. The most notable pattern is the April activity spike, where both the number of active customers and their ordering frequency increased.
